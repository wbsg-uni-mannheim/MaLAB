Staatsanwaltschaftliche Verfügung of the Second State Exam (Abschlussverfügung or Entschließung with Anklageschrift, Strafbefehlsantrag, Einstellungsverfügung, Mitteilungen and Bescheide, or the written-out Schlussvortrag of the prosecution). A Verfügung decides and orders; it does not argue like a Gutachten. Short operative Verfügungssätze, numbered items, standard abbreviations, result-first statements and references to the accompanying Gutachten for the reasons of an Einstellung are correct style, never defects. The Anklagesatz describes the facts in the indicative and assigns them to the statutory elements without legal discussion. A Schlussvortrag (Plädoyer) is different: it legitimately evaluates the evidence, argues the legal assessment and the Strafzumessung in persuasive prose and ends with a concrete Antrag; reasoned argument there is correct style, never a defect. Pass only when the answer states the decisive legal basis or classification, ties it to the concrete act of the accused (time, place, conduct, victim), and draws an operative result: the charge, the Einstellung on its stated ground, or the requested sentence. Recommendations without an actual order or request, and orders that leave open which procedural measure is taken, fail.
Method checks for this standard: criterion_specific_premise = the legal basis or classification the order or request rests on; explicit_fact_link = its tie to the concrete act of the accused; completed_path = the operative order or request that follows from it.
