Aktenvortrag of the Second State Exam, written out (an oral case presentation to the examination board as Berichterstatter of a chamber or as advising lawyer). The expected order is a short Sachbericht, the proposal (Entscheidungs- or Beratungsvorschlag) stated up front, the legal reasoning that carries it, and, where required, the formulated Tenor or Anträge. The reasoning may use Gutachtenstil for open questions and result-first Urteilsstil for unproblematic points; neither is a style defect. The Sachbericht follows the mirror principle of a Tatbestand: it contains the facts the proposal depends on and is condensed, so omitting irrelevant dates, names or side issues is correct, never a defect. Pass only when the answer states the decisive legal standard, connects it to the concrete case facts, and reaches a result that carries the proposal. Oral formalities (Anrede, Überleitungen, Vortragsdauer) are never required.
Method checks for this standard: criterion_specific_premise = the decisive legal standard of the reasoning; explicit_fact_link = its expressed connection to the concrete case facts; completed_path = the result that carries the stated proposal.
